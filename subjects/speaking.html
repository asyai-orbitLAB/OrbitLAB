Speaking & listening
with confidence.

Find your voice in class discussions, make presentations that hold attention, and get more comfortable following spoken English.

Make space for your ideas

Good communication is a two-way journey. Practise sharing your thoughts clearly, listening for what matters, and asking questions that move a conversation forward.

Explore the essentials

  • Enter a discussion and build on someone’s point
  • Use useful phrases to agree, disagree, and clarify
  • Plan and deliver a confident short presentation
  • Listen for main ideas, detail, and speaker attitude
  • Practise pronunciation without losing your natural voice

Curiosity keeps
conversation moving.

When you’re not sure what to say next, ask a thoughtful follow-up question. “What makes you say that?” is a great place to start.
