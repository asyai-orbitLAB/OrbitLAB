Vocabulary in context
words that work.

Learn words by seeing how they work in real sentences, then practise choosing the right expression for your meaning and audience.

Make new words yours

A useful vocabulary grows through noticing and practice. Pay attention to the words around a new expression: the patterns, partners, and situations that give it meaning.

  • Notice collocations and common word partnerships
  • Choose a suitable academic tone and register
